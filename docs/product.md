# mpanfolio Product Brief

## Purpose

mpanfolio is a personal portfolio that presents the site owner as a strong candidate for relevant employment. It gives visitors a fast, credible view of professional value, experience, skills, and selected work.

## Target visitor

Primary visitors are hiring managers and recruiters evaluating candidates for relevant roles. Secondary visitors are prospective collaborators and professional contacts.

Visitors may arrive with little context and limited time. The home page must make the owner's role, strengths, and evidence of work clear without requiring extra navigation.

## Primary visitor action

Contact the site owner to discuss a role or interview. Contact access must be visible, direct, and require no account or custom backend.

## Launch sections

The launch site uses `/` as a complete portfolio with these sections:

1. **Hero** — public name, role, concise positioning statement, and primary contact action.
2. **Selected work** — strongest public projects or case studies, including the problem, contribution, result, and relevant link.
3. **Experience** — roles, organizations, dates, and outcome-focused highlights.
4. **Skills** — relevant, defensible skills grouped for quick scanning.
5. **Contact** — direct contact method and useful professional links.
6. **Footer** — essential navigation and professional links.

## Launch non-goals

- Blog, post authoring, tags, RSS, search, or syntax highlighting.
- CMS, database, authentication, admin UI, or custom contact backend.
- Separate project routes unless launch content proves the home page is insufficient.
- Analytics without a specific measurement question.
- Decorative animation that delays or obscures core information.
- Publishing private, confidential, outdated, or unsupported claims.

A future blog may use repository Markdown or MDX after at least one real post is ready. Blog work must not delay portfolio launch.

## Launch success criteria

Launch is successful when:

- A first-time visitor can identify the owner's professional role and value within the first screen.
- The site shows credible evidence through selected work and outcome-focused experience.
- A visitor can reach the primary contact action from the first screen and contact section.
- Every public claim, date, link, and contact detail is accurate and safe to publish.
- The experience works with keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings, and approximately 390 px and 1440 px viewports.
- The production build completes without errors, and the deployed site has no broken internal or external links.
- The site is deployed at a stable public URL and can be shared with a prospective employer.
